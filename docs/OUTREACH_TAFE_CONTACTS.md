## Caveats
- TAFE NSW is the only provider with an enquiry form that is explicitly tagged to the CPC40120 course code rather than a fully general contact channel.
- CIT (ACT) and TasTAFE course/contact pages blocked direct automated fetch (HTTP 403); the contact details above came from indexed search snippets of those same domains, not a direct page read — worth a manual browser check before emailing.
- South Metropolitan TAFE: could not confirm they currently deliver CPC40120 at all; only the Diploma (CPC50220) has a live course page. Worth a phone call to confirm before drafting an email.
- None of the ten providers published a named individual as the course/department contact — all routes are role-based (forms, general phone lines, or department inboxes), consistent with the brief's instruction not to include individual names unless the provider itself publishes them.
