# TAFE / RTO teacher outreach — draft

Plan agreed 16/09/26: offer the tools free to building and construction teachers (CPC40120 Certificate IV in Building and Construction covers reading plans and estimating/monitoring costs). The ask is "is this useful for your students?", not "please promote me". Vish sends each email himself, from his own address, in his own words. Nothing here has been sent.

## Who

Head teachers / course coordinators for Building and Construction at TAFE NSW, TAFE Queensland, Holmesglen, Box Hill, TAFE SA, North Metropolitan TAFE (WA), plus private RTOs running CPC40120. Use the contact published on each institute's building course page; don't scrape personal emails.

Start with 5 and see if any reply before sending more.

## Email draft

**Subject:** Free estimating tools for your Cert IV Building students

Hi [name],

I'm an estimator in shopfitting and commercial fitout in [city]. Over the last year I've built a set of free browser tools for the everyday estimating jobs: a cut list optimiser, a critical-path programme builder, material quantity calculators (plasterboard, tiles, concrete, paint) and a PDF toolkit for comparing drawing revisions.

They're at vishvaddi.com/site. They're free, need no account, and run on a phone or a lab PC without installing anything.

I thought they might be useful for students working through the estimating and planning units, either as a check on their manual calculations or to see a programme's critical path update as they change links.

If you have five minutes to look, I'd genuinely like to know what's wrong or missing from a teaching point of view. I'm happy to adjust things for a class, and there's no catch: students never need to pay for anything.

Cheers,
Vish Vaddi
