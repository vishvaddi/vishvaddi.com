# About

I'm Vish. I live in Sydney and work as an estimator pricing premium retail shopfitting projects.

Outside of work I listen to and produce music, watch films and YouTube videos — especially about physics, astronomy, and science in general. I read. I spend a fair amount of time thinking about bushcraft, survival, and self-sufficiency. I love learning, and I have a deep appreciation for art in all its forms.

This site is the bit of the internet I own. The professional side is at [/work](/work). Notes I think are worth keeping go in [/notes](/notes). Longer writing in [/blog](/blog). The interest sections — [music](/music), [movies](/movies), [books](/books), [prepping](/prepping) — are slowly filling in.
