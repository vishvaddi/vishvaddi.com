# Work

I'm an estimator in Sydney. I price premium retail and commercial fit-out: shopfronts, joinery and finishes packages, from first-pass budgets through to tender and variation pricing. The job is turning drawings and a scope into quantities, rates and a programme that a builder can stand behind.

## Tools that came out of the job

Each of these started as a spreadsheet or a scrap of paper that kept breaking. They run in the browser, need no account, and keep your data on your device. They are free; [Pro](/pro) puts your own brand on the client-facing exports.

- **[Cut List Optimiser](/site/cut-list)** — nests parts onto sheets and lengths so the material order matches the drawings, with a printable cutting plan.
- **[Programme Builder](/site/programme)** — a critical-path Gantt in working days, with procurement order-by dates and baseline variance, built for fit-out durations rather than year-long civil jobs.
- **[Rate Builder](/site/rate) and [Charge-Out Rate](/site/charge-rate)** — labour rates from wages, on-costs, overhead and margin, so a quoted hour covers what it costs.
- **[Calculator and Notepad](/site/calc)** — takeoff maths that stays traceable: variables, units, GST, percentages and running totals in plain lines you can print or paste.
- **[PDF Toolkit](/site/pdf)** — stamp, split, extract and compare drawing sets without uploading them anywhere.
- **[Material calculators](/site/materials)** — plasterboard, paint, tiles, concrete and timber spans, plus quantity answers for common rooms.

The full list is on the [Site tools](/site) hub. The [Audio tools](/audio) and the [Studio](/studio) came from the other half of my week.

## How I build

Plain web technology on Cloudflare's edge: no frameworks in the browser, no accounts, no analytics, a strict content-security policy, and nothing you type leaves the page unless a tool says so. Every tool gets a browser test harness before it ships. The same discipline goes into the desktop estimating tooling I'm developing separately: AI-assisted scope generation and quote comparison for Australian estimators.
