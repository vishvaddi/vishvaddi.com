title: "Domain expertise is the moat AI cannot replicate"
description: "As AI tools lower the barrier to building software, deep industry knowledge becomes the differentiator that compounds, not the one that erodes."
pubDate: 2026-03-11
tags: ["ai", "estimating", "construction", "moats"]

As AI tools lower the barrier to building software, the differentiator shifts
entirely to domain knowledge. Anyone can generate a generic app. Very few
people can identify a real, painful problem in a specific industry, describe
it precisely, and build a targeted solution for a known audience.

Deep domain knowledge provides three compounding advantages:

1. **Problem identification.** You see pain points others miss because you've
   lived them.
2. **Solution credibility.** Your industry network trusts your product because
   you have a track record.
3. **Distribution head start.** You already know the people who have the
   problem.

In construction, estimating, and shopfitting — where most software is built
by people who have never set foot on a site — insider knowledge is not a small
advantage. It is an enormous one.

The risk is spending years building skills AI will absorb, while neglecting
the domain expertise AI cannot replicate. The correct bet is to deepen the
domain knowledge, and let AI tools amplify its reach.
