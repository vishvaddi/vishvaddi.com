The good ideas I want to keep deserve a home that will outlast whichever
social network is in vogue this decade. LinkedIn, Twitter, a Notion page — none
of them are mine. This one is.

The plan is small. A clear professional page for the estimating side of my
life. A notes section for the writing side. A `/now` page I actually update.
No comments, no analytics that follow you around, no cookie banner because
there's nothing to consent to.

If you're a recruiter or a client, [/work](/work) is for you. If you're here
because you found one of my notes, welcome — there's more on the way.

— Vish
