This is the first post on a site that's been promised to itself for years.

I'm building it because the alternatives — LinkedIn, Twitter, a Notion page —
are platforms I don't own. The good ideas I want to keep deserve a home that
will outlast whichever social network is in vogue this decade.

The plan is small. A clear professional page for the estimating side of my
life. A notes section for the writing side. A `/now` page that I actually
update. No comments, no analytics that follow you around, no cookie banner
because there's nothing to consent to.

If you're a recruiter or a client, the [/work](/work) page is for you. If
you're here because you found one of my notes, welcome — there's more on the
way.

— Vish
